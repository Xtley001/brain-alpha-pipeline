# Strategy Module: Synthetic Forward Basis Spread

## Theoretical Edge
Under put-call parity, synthetic forward prices reflect market expectations of future spot levels including dividend adjustments and carrying financing rates. Persistent cross-sectional dispersion in forward-to-spot basis predicts equity return drift over 20–60 day holding horizons.

## Optimal Execution Parameters
* **Universes:** `TOP2000`, `TOP1000`, `TOP3000`
* **Holding Decays:** 18 to 26 Days
* **Neutralizations:** `SUBINDUSTRY`, `SECTOR`, `INDUSTRY`
