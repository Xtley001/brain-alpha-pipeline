# Strategy Module: Cross-Asset Hybrid Confluence

## Theoretical Edge
Asset prices do not adjust instantaneously across fragmented financial markets. Options market participants frequently trade ahead of cash equity participants, while equity lending supply (borrow fees) reflects institutional conviction that equity analysts take weeks to digest. Cross-asset hybrid confluence synthesizes orthogonal signals from all three dimensions, delivering higher Sharpe ratios with minimal market factor beta.

## Optimal Execution Parameters
* **Universes:** `TOPSP500`, `TOP1000`, `TOP2000`
* **Holding Decays:** 18 to 26 Days
* **Neutralizations:** `SUBINDUSTRY`, `SECTOR`, `MARKET`
